# BUKHARA · بخارا — Art Direction
## "Dawn over the Registan" — a Samarkand-turquoise nikah invitation

### Concept
A silk-road dawn. The hero is a gradient sky — lapis into turquoise into
saffron at the horizon — over a turquoise ribbed dome, framed by a
Persian pishtaq arch of glazed tile. The page is ivory silk between
lagoons of girih tilework. The visual language is Timurid-Persian:
glazed tile roundels, eslimi arabesque vines, palmette rules, Persian
tulips. Premium transparent PNG art throughout — never clip-art.

### Readability law (the noor lesson)
Raw saffron is never body text on light paper; text on ivory uses ink,
ink-soft, deep teal or gold-ink. Saffron text is reserved for dark
lagoon grounds. Ornament and hairlines may use saffron anywhere.

### Type
- Display: **Italiana** — the silk-road's thin luxury voice
- Editorial: **Cormorant Garamond** — traveller's prose
- Script: **Alex Brush** — the couple's names
- Arabic: **Amiri** · display **Aref Ruqaa** · UI **Jost** · hand **Caveat**

### Cinematic grammar
- Dawn-sky hero gradient with a saffron horizon glow.
- Girih tile texture on every dark band, dimmed for parchment text.
- Sun-disc shamsa behind the arch; rising light motes.
- Letterbox bars over the envelope that part on the seal.
- Film grain at 4% multiply; slow light sweep on dark-band titles.

### Sections
Envelope → dawn hero (pishtaq arch, dome, Ar-Rum 30:21) → story ivory
letter with An-Nahl 16:72 plate → girih ayat band → save-the-date →
five Muslim events → palette/particulars → itinerary → venue →
countdown → RSVP → closing dua with the turquoise girih medallion seal.

### Rules
- Premium PNG art via the white-key flood-fill cutout pipeline.
- Arabic is real webfont text — never AI calligraphy images.
- Turquoise stays GLAZED (bright cyan-teal); never navy, never grey.
